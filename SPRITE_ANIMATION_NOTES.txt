CTF sprite animation update

Assets (src/ctf/ui):
- runner.gif: used for idle/running character frames.
- jumper.gif: included and loaded for the future jump/gravity implementation; it is not activated yet because the current game has no jump/vertical physics state.

Current behavior:
- The old red/blue player dots are replaced by the runner character sprite.
- The first runner frame is used while a player is stationary.
- The runner frames animate while the server-reported player position changes.
- The sprite flips horizontally when the player moves left.
- Team-coloured ground markers and a yellow outline for the local player remain.
- Server/client movement and the lobby protocol are unchanged.

Resource files must remain under src/ctf/ui so they are copied into the classpath by the NetBeans/Ant build.
